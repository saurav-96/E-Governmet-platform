**Your city, right at your fingertips.**

## So, what is this?

Ever wished dealing with your local government felt as easy as scrolling through your phone? That's the idea behind **Butwal SmartGov**.

It's a digital home for Butwal Sub-Metropolitan City, a single place where people can see what's going on in their city, find the services they need, and actually have a say. No long queues, no confusing paperwork, and no guessing who to ask.

## What you'll find inside

- 🏥 **Health, Education & Agriculture**: what's happening in the sectors that touch everyday life
- 🌱 **A Greener City**: waste management, green transport, urban drainage, and who's accountable for what
- 🚦 **Traffic & Safety**: keeping the roads safer for everyone
- 💼 **Business & Tourism**: what's growing in Butwal, and why it's worth a visit
- 💡 **IT & Innovation**: where the city is headed next
- 📊 **Live Dashboards & Reports**: real numbers and clear charts, with nothing hidden
- 🤖 **AI Assistant**: got a question? Just ask, any time
- 📅 **Events & Updates**: never miss what's happening around town

## Why we built it

We think good governance starts with being open. When people can see the progress, the plans, and the problems, they can trust the system more and help make it better. This project is our small step toward a smarter, friendlier, and more connected Butwal. 💙

## Built with

React · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Three.js · GSAP · Framer Motion · Recharts
